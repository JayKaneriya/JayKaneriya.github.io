01 Context

Zimdle is a SaaS platform for structured private customer feedback and business performance visibility — so owners can monitor trends without relying only on public social-media reviews.

The local codebase is Laravel 5.3 with Stripe Cashier subscriptions, AJAX dashboard charts, DomPDF reports, Twilio SMS alerts, criteria/templates and enterprise comparison tools.

02 Product visuals

Illustrative 4:3 mockups of the feedback SaaS surfaces verified in the codebase.

03 Responsibility

  • Built a structured private feedback workflow that routes responses to the business owner and representatives.
  • Implemented dashboard analytics: feedback history, criteria averages, time-period charts and competition/benchmark charts.
  • Added PDF reports, email/SMS alerts for low averages, and scheduled report jobs.
  • Shipped Stripe subscription plans (subscribe, change, resume, unsubscribe) gated behind subscription middleware.
  • Delivered AJAX-driven UI for templates, criteria, business profiles, galleries and enterprise compare.

04 Challenges

  • Keeping feedback private to owners while still enabling actionable analytics.
  • Surfacing trends, history and alerts in a way operators can act on quickly.
  • Combining SaaS billing, multi-business ownership and enterprise comparison in one product surface.

05 Solution

Private feedback capture, criteria templates, chart endpoints, PDF/email reporting and Twilio alerts — so owners get structured insight without public social blowback.
