01 Context

Zimdle is a SaaS platform for structured private customer feedback and business performance visibility — so owners can monitor trends without relying only on public social-media reviews.

02 Responsibility

  • Built a structured private feedback workflow that routes responses to the business owner.
  • Implemented feedback history, statistics, visual charts and trend monitoring.
  • Added reports, alerts and business-performance comparisons for ongoing monitoring.
  • Delivered an AJAX-driven application UI with Laravel, CodeIgniter, JavaScript, jQuery, Sass and Bootstrap.

03 Challenges

  • Keeping feedback private to the owner while still enabling actionable analytics.
  • Surfacing trends, history and alerts in a way operators can act on quickly.
  • Building a maintainable AJAX-driven SaaS UI across Laravel and CodeIgniter surfaces.

04 Solution

Private feedback capture, history/statistics, charts, reports and alerts — so owners get structured insight without public social blowback.

05 Outcomes

Full-stack SaaS delivery around structured feedback, analytics, reporting and operational alerts — focused on problem, contribution and implementation rather than unverified metrics.

06 Technology

  • Stack: Laravel, CodeIgniter, JavaScript, jQuery, AJAX, Sass, Bootstrap, Git, JSON
  • Product surface: private feedback, history/statistics, charts, reports, alerts, performance comparison
