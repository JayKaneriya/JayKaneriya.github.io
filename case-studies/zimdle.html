Problem

Public reviews are blunt instruments. Owners need honest customer signal — ratings, comments, and trends — without fear of social media execution when one bad night goes viral.

Approach

  • Built a private feedback channel that routes structured responses straight to the business owner.
  • Shipped history stats, visual charts, and a trend engine so patterns show up before they become crises.
  • Added automated reports, alerts, and performance benchmarking for day-to-day ops.
  • Delivered full-stack with Laravel, CodeIgniter, and AJAX-driven UI (Sass / Bootstrap).

Outcome

A real-time feedback and performance monitoring product that helps businesses improve from the customer's perspective — privately, with charts and alerts instead of public pile-ons.
