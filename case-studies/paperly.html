01 / OVERVIEW

Behind the build.

Paperly is a school management platform used by Australian schools to replace paper trails, manual spreadsheets and fragmented communication. The system coordinates complex school operations: multi-day excursions, parent consent, dynamic form builders, calendar events, medical tracking and parent-teacher interviews.

Schools need strict role-scoped workflows: teachers draft requests, heads of department approve them, finance checks budgets, and parents submit legally binding consent with emergency contact details.

02 / MY ROLE

Senior Developer Ownership

As Senior Web Application Developer working remotely with Paperly’s Perth-based engineering team, I took end-to-end responsibility for key platform modules from architecture to production deployments.

  • Designed relational data schemas and Eloquent models for excursions, forms, permissions and scheduling.
  • Built high-throughput REST APIs consumed by Vue.js staff, parent and student portals.
  • Implemented multi-step approval workflows with audit logs and automated email/SMS reminders.
  • Handled regular production releases, hotfixes, data migrations and cross-time-zone team collaboration.
03 / KEY MODULES BUILT

Core Platform Workflows

  • Digital Excursions: Complete planning workflows with risk assessments, transport booking, student rosters, medical alerts and parent permission tracking.
  • Dynamic Form Builder: Configurable form engine supporting custom field types, conditional logic, multi-stage approvals and exportable responses.
  • Parent Approvals & Consent: Mobile-friendly portal where parents review excursion details, confirm medical data, and provide digital sign-off.
  • Calendars & Parent-Teacher Interviews: Time-slot scheduling engine preventing booking conflicts for hundreds of families.
04 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Modular Laravel & Vue Architecture.

Role-scoped web portals share a unified modular Laravel backend, managing school-scoped databases, external Student Information System sync, and notification dispatch.

Architectural diagram: Role portals interface through modular Laravel endpoints with MySQL, SIS abstraction, SSO identity, and queued notifications.

05 / INTEGRATIONS

School Ecosystem Sync

School data sync is abstracted behind a clean adapter interface so platform modules run consistently against different SIS/LMS platforms. Supported ecosystem integrations include:

  • Synergetic & TASS: Bi-directional student enrolment, parent contact and timetable synchronization.
  • Veracross, SEQTA & Schoolbox: Class rosters, medical flags and teacher scheduling.
  • Microsoft Identity & Wonde: Enterprise single sign-on (SSO) and automated identity federation.
06 / SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

Data Protection & Roles

School environments demand strict privacy boundaries. Medical details, consent slips, and dietary restrictions are isolated with per-school tenant safeguards:

  • Role-scoped permissions ensuring teachers only access assigned student cohorts.
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive parent declarations and medical documents.
  • Detailed audit trail tracking who viewed, approved, or modified operational records.
