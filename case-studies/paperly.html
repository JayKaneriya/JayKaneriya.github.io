Paperly — K-12 school operations at scale

Product context

Paperly is a cloud-based K-12 school operations and workflow platform. It helps schools replace fragmented manual processes with connected workflows for administration, compliance, approvals, forms, communication and activities. Public product information lists trips & excursions, digital forms, compliance, risk assessments and announcements among its tools.

My role

I joined the product team as a Senior Web Application Developer and worked remotely with the Perth-based team from July 2022 through August 2026. This is my strongest recent production experience. My work focused on Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL and REST APIs in a live multi-school environment.

Contribution

  • Production Laravel/PHP and Vue.js feature development.
  • MySQL / database-driven application work and REST API integrations.
  • Dynamic forms and workflow automation.
  • Excursion and activity workflows.
  • Permissions, approvals and geolocation-enabled workflows.
  • Debugging, testing and ongoing production support in an Agile environment.

Technical focus

  • Backend: Laravel, PHP, MySQL, REST APIs
  • Frontend: Vue.js, JavaScript
  • Application concerns: permissions, workflows, forms, integrations, geolocation and production delivery

Product scale

Paperly supports independent schools, government schools, Catholic/Diocesan systems and multi-campus organisations. Its public platform information describes digital forms, trips and excursions, student details, compliance, announcements, approvals and integrations with SIS/LMS/identity systems. Paperly states that more than 250 new schools joined for 2026 and beyond.

Outcome

The work demonstrates senior production experience with complex, workflow-heavy EdTech software — not only a technology list. My contribution was part of a live platform used daily by thousands of teachers and parents, where permissions, data accuracy, integrations and reliability matter.
