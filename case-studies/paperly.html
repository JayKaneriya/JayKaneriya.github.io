01 Context

Paperly is a cloud-based K-12 school operations and workflow platform. It helps schools replace fragmented manual processes with connected workflows for administration, compliance, approvals, forms, communication and activities. Public product information lists trips & excursions, digital forms, compliance, risk assessments and announcements among its tools.

Paperly supports independent schools, government schools, Catholic/Diocesan systems and multi-campus organisations. Paperly states that more than 250 new schools joined for 2026 and beyond.

02 Responsibility

I joined as a Senior Web Application Developer and worked remotely with the Perth-based team from July 2022 through August 2026. This is my strongest recent production experience. My work focused on Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL and REST APIs in a live multi-school environment.

  • Production Laravel/PHP and Vue.js feature development.
  • MySQL / database-driven application work and REST API integrations.
  • Dynamic forms and workflow automation.
  • Excursion and activity workflows.
  • Permissions, approvals and geolocation-enabled workflows.
  • Debugging, testing and ongoing production support in an Agile environment.

03 Challenges

  • Multi-school permissions and data accuracy where reliability matters daily.
  • Workflow-heavy features (forms, approvals, excursions) that must stay usable for non-technical staff.
  • Integrations and geolocation-enabled processes in a live production product.
  • Remote collaboration across timezones with a Perth-based product team.

04 Solution

Delivered school administration modules end-to-end: Laravel backends, Vue.js interfaces, REST APIs, dynamic forms, workflow automation, geolocation and multi-school permissions — shipped and supported in Agile production releases.

05 Outcomes

Stable multi-year delivery on a live platform used daily by thousands of teachers and parents. The work demonstrates senior production experience with complex, workflow-heavy EdTech software — not only a technology list.

06 Technology

  • Backend: Laravel, PHP, MySQL, REST APIs
  • Frontend: Vue.js, JavaScript
  • Application concerns: permissions, workflows, forms, integrations, geolocation and production delivery
