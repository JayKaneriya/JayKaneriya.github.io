01 Context

Resident App is a personal multi-tenant society management platform — replacing WhatsApp groups and paper registers with structured notices, maintenance requests, visitor flows, amenity booking and billing for residents, guards and society admins.

The local codebase is Laravel 11 with Inertia/React web panels, Sanctum APIs, stancl/tenancy, Stripe Cashier + Razorpay, Spatie permissions and an Expo React Native mobile app for residents and guards.

02 Product visuals

Illustrative 4:3 mockups of the society surfaces verified in the codebase — admin ops, mobile/web parity and platform flow.

03 Responsibility

  • Owned the full product surface — domain modeling, Laravel APIs, Inertia/React admin & resident panels and Expo mobile.
  • Built resident workflows for notices, maintenance/complaints, amenity slots, invoices and AI support Q&A.
  • Shipped ops tooling for society staff: assignment, status updates, visitor QR scanning, SOS alerts and audit logging.
  • Implemented multi-tenancy, role permissions, notification routing (push → SMS → email) and payment integrations.
  • Added enterprise-leaning foundations: SAML SSO config hooks, workflow engine, late-fee jobs and global search.

04 Challenges

  • Replacing chat chaos with structured notices and request tracking that non-technical users will actually use.
  • Keeping resident, guard and admin experiences role-appropriate across web and mobile.
  • Hardening multi-tenant isolation, PII handling and payment/billing correctness for high-trust society ops.

05 Solution

Tenant-scoped societies with clear roles, omni-channel parity for resident features, queued notifications and payment rails — so community ops stay auditable and mobile-first.
