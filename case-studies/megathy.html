Problem

Eastern Saudi Arabia needed on-demand grocery that could handle many vendors, scheduled slots, payments, and live delivery status — not a single-store checkout bolted onto a brochure site.

Approach

  • Built the Laravel backend, admin panel, and order management used for day-to-day ops.
  • Shipped a multi-vendor product catalog with real-time order tracking and scheduled delivery slots.
  • Integrated multi-payment processing, discounts, live chat support, and a reward / loyalty layer.
  • Delivered Vue.js / jQuery admin UI on MySQL with Sass and Bootstrap for reliable ops workflows.

Outcome

A production grocery platform serving thousands of customers across Eastern Saudi Arabia — web, iOS, and Android — with vendor catalogs, live tracking, and payment flows that hold up under real order volume.
